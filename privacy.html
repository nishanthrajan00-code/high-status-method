Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information High Status Method collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account and checkout information: your email address and payment details, collected and processed by our payment provider, Whop.
  • AI conversations: messages you send to Nish AI, processed by our AI chat provider, Chatbase, to generate responses. Conversations may be reviewed to improve the quality of Nish AI's answers.
  • Communications: anything you send us directly, such as support emails or content submitted for review.
  • Usage information: basic technical data (like browser type and general location) automatically logged by our hosting provider, Netlify, for security and reliability purposes. Our payment provider, Whop, also runs its own conversion-tracking pixel on this site to measure checkout performance.
  • Analytics: pages visited, links and buttons clicked, and general session behavior, collected through Google Analytics (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity to help us understand site usage and improve the Service. Microsoft Clarity may also record anonymized session replays and click/scroll heatmaps.

We do not receive or store your full card number. Payments are handled entirely by Whop, which maintains its own security and compliance standards.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your membership access
  • To power Nish AI's responses to your questions
  • To send receipts, access details, and important account notices
  • To respond to support requests
  • To review content you submit for feedback inside the membership
  • To understand how visitors use the site and improve its design and performance

3. Third Parties

We share information only as needed to operate the Service: with Whop for payment processing, membership access, and its own conversion tracking; with Chatbase to power Nish AI conversations; with Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity for site analytics and session recordings; with Netlify for website hosting; and with Google Fonts to load on-page typography. We do not sell your information.

4. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide the Membership, respond to support requests, and meet legal or accounting obligations.

5. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing me@bynish.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to me@bynish.com.
